Manage forms of every installed host, publish web forms, read their
submissions and manage their destinations.

<form> is a form id, slug or name. <rule> is a destination's id or name.

Discover what you can use:
  tinycld form hosts                     hosts, their field kinds and built-in fields
  tinycld form example definition        a valid starting definition
  tinycld form fields <form>             a form's field keys and {{fields.<key>}} placeholders
  tinycld form destination actions       actions a destination can run, with their params
  tinycld form example destination       a valid starting destination

Every list and view honors --output table|json|csv. A refused change lists
every problem with its path; fix them all, then send the file again.
