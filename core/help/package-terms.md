## What this does

Some packages come with terms and conditions. Nobody in the workspace can use such a package until the **owner** agrees to its terms. Until then the package has no entry for members and admins, and its settings, help topics and search results do not show. The owner keeps the package's entry in the navigation.

## To accept the terms of a package

Only the owner can accept terms.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Package terms**.
2. Find the package and press **Review**.
3. Read the terms, then press **Agree**.

The package opens for everybody at once. You can also open the package from the navigation: the owner sees the terms there, with **Agree** and **Decline**.

When you start the app and a package still waits for your decision, the start page opens **Package terms** first. It does this one time each time you start the app, not each time you go to the start page. A link that goes straight to a page does not do this.

Press **Not now** to decide later. This records nothing. The app opens **Package terms** again the next time you start the app. It does this until you agree or decline.

## During setup

In the setup guide, the **Choose your apps** step marks an app that has terms. Press the app to read the terms. **Agree** turns the app on. **Not now** leaves it off; you can accept later in **Settings**.

## If you decline

Press **Decline** to record that you do not agree. The package stays closed and the app does not ask again at the start page. To accept later, open **Settings**, then **Package terms**, press **Review**, then **Agree**.

## When the terms change

When an update of a package brings new terms, the package closes again until the owner agrees to the new version. The app asks the owner at the start page.

## To read terms you accepted

Open **Settings**, then **Package terms**, and press **Read** on the package. Press **Close** when you finish.

## If you are not the owner

When you open a link to a package whose terms are not accepted, you see **Waiting for the owner to accept the terms**. Ask the owner of your workspace to open the package.

## What is recorded

Each decision (agree or decline) is stored with the package, the version of the terms, the owner's account and the time. It also shows in the audit log.
