## Your profile

Open **Settings → Profile** to update the name and recovery email on your
account. Changes save automatically when you move out of a field.

The recovery email is where invitations and password reset links go. You do not
sign in with it. Sign in with your username, or with an email address this
server hosts for you.

## Changing your password

In **Settings → Security**, select **Change password**:

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Enter a **new password** (at least 8 characters).
3. Re-enter it to **confirm**.
4. Select **Save**.

You stay signed in after the change — there's no need to log back in. If
[two-step verification](help://core:security-mfa) is on, type a code from your
authenticator app to finish. If you mistype
your current password, you'll see an error and your password won't be changed.

## Disabling your account

If you want to step away but might come back, disable your account instead of
deleting it. In **Settings → Account access**, select
**Disable my account** and type your email to confirm.

Disabling takes effect immediately: you're signed out everywhere, you can't sign
in, and links you've shared stop working. Your content stays exactly where it is,
and mail sent to your mailbox keeps arriving — it's waiting for you when the
account is re-enabled. Senders aren't told the account is suspended.

Only an organization **owner or admin** can re-enable a disabled account. After
re-enabling, sign in again with your usual password — earlier sessions aren't
restored.

## Deleting your account

Deleting is **permanent and cannot be undone**. In **Settings → Account access**,
select **Delete my account**. Your email and password are removed and you're
signed out everywhere. Your name stays on the work you leave in place.

Before confirming, choose what happens to your files, documents and comments:

- **Reassign to another member** — ownership transfers to the teammate you pick;
  nothing is lost.
- **Delete everything** — permanently removes your content along with the
  account.

If there's nobody to reassign to, your content stays in place attributed to
you. Type your email to confirm the deletion.

If you are the only owner of something that other people use, and you do not
reassign your content, the deletion is refused. The message names the item. Make another member an owner,
or delete the item, then delete your account again.
